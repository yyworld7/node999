Who We Are

Earth Guardians is a youth-led climate action network committed to bold solutions and grassroots impact.

Our Purpose & Promise

Earth Guardians was born in 2013 from a simple belief: that everyday people have the power to reverse environmental damage. We combine local action with global advocacy to demand climate justice, restore natural habitats, and hold polluters accountable.

What started as a small group of students in Portland, Oregon has grown into a coalition of over 85,000 volunteers, campaigners, scientists, and educators operating in 95+ countries.

We don't just talk about change — we build it. From legal challenges against deforestation to urban rewilding projects, every campaign is rooted in science and powered by community.

Our Programs

Targeted initiatives that tackle the root causes of ecological breakdown and deliver measurable impact.

Forest & Land Restoration

Reversing deforestation by working with Indigenous communities and local farmers to replant native woodlands, stabilize soil, and revive watersheds across five continents.

Explore Program

Coastal & Marine Defense

Fortifying coastlines through mangrove restoration, coral reef monitoring, and community-led campaigns to eliminate single-use plastics from oceans and waterways.

Explore Program

Community Clean Energy

Installing solar microgrids in underserved neighborhoods, training local technicians, and advocating for policies that accelerate a just transition away from fossil fuels.
